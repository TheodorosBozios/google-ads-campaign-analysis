# Google Ads Campaign Performance Analysis

Marketing Analytics case study analyzing five Google Ads campaigns for an e-commerce business. The goal is to evaluate efficiency with key marketing KPIs, identify underperformers, and propose a data-driven budget reallocation and 3-month optimization plan.

## Key Findings

- **Best ROAS:** Display – Awareness (6.0) — strongest revenue per euro spent  
- **Best Conversion Rate:** Shopping – Accessories (5.0%)  
- **Highest CTR:** Search – Electronics (3.0%)  
- **Below target ROAS (3.0):** Search – Apparel (2.8) and Video – Engagement (1.0)  
- **~33.9%** of total budget is spent on campaigns with Conversion Rate below 3%

## Recommendations

**Immediate**
- Improve Quality Score and ad–landing page relevance for **Search – Apparel** (reduce CPC, protect conversions)
- Cut budget on **Video – Engagement** and reallocate to higher-ROAS campaigns; improve hooks, CTA and targeting; run A/B tests
- Protect / slightly increase spend on **Display – Awareness** and **Shopping – Accessories**

**3-month plan (same total budget)**
1. **Month 1:** Shift budget from Video → Shopping Accessories + Search Electronics  
2. **Month 2:** A/B test landing pages; redesign Search – Apparel ads  
3. **Month 3:** Apply algorithmic bidding on Search & Shopping; weekly ROAS / conversion monitoring  

**Target:** ~**+15% total conversions** without increasing overall spend.
